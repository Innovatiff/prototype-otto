# Otto Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2026_

Otto is a personal voice assistant. This document describes exactly what
Otto does with your data — and what it never does.

## Health data

Otto integrates with Apple Health only if you allow it, and every part of a
guided session works identically if you decline.

**What Otto reads** (with your permission): workouts, active energy, body
mass, sleep analysis, and steps — to personalize your training plans and
daily briefings.

**What Otto writes**: completed guided workouts (duration and estimated
active energy), so your Health activity history stays complete.

**What never happens with health data:**

- It is **never stored on Otto's servers**, in iCloud, or in any
  third-party backend. Health data stays on your device.
- If a specific number (for example, last night's sleep) is used to answer
  a question, it is sent as **ephemeral context for that single request
  only** and is **never persisted server-side** — not in conversation
  history, not in memory, not in logs.
- It is **never used for advertising or analytics** of any kind.

## Voice

Speech is transcribed **on your device**. Only the resulting text of what
you said to Otto is sent to Otto's server to produce a response.

## What Otto stores

- **Owner memories** — durable facts you tell Otto to remember (or that it
  learns from conversation), always visible and editable by you.
- **Tasks, reminders, and plans** you create.
- **Short-lived conversation context** (about 30 minutes) so follow-ups
  make sense. Conversations are not kept as chat history.

All stored data is scoped to your account, readable by you alone, and used
only to operate Otto for you.

## What Otto never does

- No selling of data, no advertising, no third-party analytics.
- Messages are drafted but **never sent** without you tapping send.
- Calendar events are written only after you confirm them.
